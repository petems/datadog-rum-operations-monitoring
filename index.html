RUM Operations Monitoring demo

An operation is a critical step in a user journey. The app calls startOperation when the step begins and succeedOperation or failOperation (error / abandoned / other) when it ends. Datadog turns these into the rum.measure.operation and rum.measure.operation.duration metrics, which you can use for monitors and SLOs.
